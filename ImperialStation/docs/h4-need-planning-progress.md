## Decisions and physical execution

An autonomous citizen selects urgent or critical needs from its own exact values,
in descending band order with a stable need-kind tie break. For each eligible need,
the Mind planner binds up to the configured K published services, orders candidates
by known travel plus activity duration, and searches actual Move and relief steps.
Core executes only the first step and replans at the next safe action boundary.
No script supplies the golden scenario's actions.

The actor view contains published service definitions and its own established
custody context. Neither current stock, current outages nor other actors' occupancy
is available to the planner. Unknown routes fail from the published/private map;
the physical topology is used only by the movement executor. A service already at
the actor's tile needs no map route. An empty or disabled cafeteria becomes known
through the citizen's failed local attempt, not a central availability broadcast.

Private failure memory records service, failed action, tick, reason and cooldown.
The next bounded search can choose an alternative. Missing alternatives produce
an explained retry clock with bounded backoff, permitting other useful work in the
meantime. An unavailable higher need can yield to another eligible urgent need.
Those searches share the request's node budget; the ordinary global request cap
still applies. Actual meal stock and capacity remain authoritative at execution.

Critical relief has priority over ordinary decisions. An urgent need defers to a
known escort/patrol obligation. Reports, check-ins, repair, other work orders,
audits and off-duty travel consult need priority before issuing competing intents.
Blocked relief yields during its retry interval. Existing finite actions retain
their safe completion behavior unless a critical threshold interrupts them.
Travel already seeking relief for the same critical need continues; a different
critical need stops travel only at the committed edge boundary. Shift boundaries
do not cancel travel belonging to a need plan. Mail still does not wake sleep.

This is a partial priority integration. Credential restoration and the complete
earliest-due priority-5 arbitration remain future work. Seek-only hunger does not
invent a meal window. Roster meals/sleep, scheduled shift breaks, optional strain
rest and social goals are not yet generated by this policy.

## Custody and actor control

Autonomous detention relief may use only the citizen's own local facility service.
An actor being escorted or awaiting intake cannot travel away for relief. Ordinary
citizens cannot choose custody-only services. Sleep binds an own assigned berth or
an authorized unassigned custody berth; ordinary vacant berth allocation remains
unfinished. Rest is not substituted for sleep.

Core NeedSeed defaults to autonomous policy for all needs-enabled actors. Its
optional autonomousActors subset permits explicit command-driven physical tests.
Runner enables the policy for actors without scripted:true; scripted actors still
accumulate needs and obey physical thresholds/service rules. The earlier
need_services fixture now explicitly marks both command-controlled actors scripted.
The new autonomous_need_relief fixture has no scripted actors or commands.

Custody integration tests reuse the earlier finite misconduct setup at ticks 0..12
to establish the later detention chain. Their relief is autonomous, but these tests
are not evidence of an entirely autonomous initial misconduct scenario.
